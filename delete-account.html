Delete your Talvii account

You can delete your Talvii account, and everything in it, at any time. There are two ways.

In the app

  1. Open Talvii and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all of its data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you've uninstalled Talvii or can't sign in, email support@talvii.co.uk from the email address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it within 7 days (usually sooner) and reply to confirm. If you no longer have that email address, say so and we'll help.

What's deleted

Everything: your email address and password, your habits, check-offs, amounts, notes, pauses and settings. We don't keep a copy. The copy on your phone is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

Want to keep your history? Before deleting, go to Settings → Export your data for spreadsheet (CSV) files.

Talvii Plus

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions, or you'll keep being charged. Apple or Google keep their own records of the payment, and RevenueCat, which checks Plus for us, keeps a record of the purchase linked to a random ID (not your email), under their own policies.

Only want to remove some data?

Deleting a habit removes it and its history. Clearing a note's text deletes the note.

See the privacy policy for what's stored and where, or the support page for other help.
