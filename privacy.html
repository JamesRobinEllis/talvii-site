Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Talvii is a habit tracker. This page explains what information the app keeps, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Your email address and password, to create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored only in hashed form; we can't read them.
  • Your habits: their names, reminder times and days, and the order you put them in.
  • Your check-offs: which habits you marked done on which days.
  • Your settings: light or dark theme and your chosen colour.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or analytics, and the app contains no ads or third-party trackers.

Why we collect it

Only to run the app: to keep your account, show your habits and history, and sync them between your devices. We don't sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything else.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Each account can only read its own data. A copy is also kept on your device so the app works offline.

Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. Reminder times never leave your device except as part of your synced habit list.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all of your habits, check-offs and settings straight away. Signing out also removes the copy kept on that device.

Children

Talvii is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
