Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support@talvii.co.uk and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days. It helps to say which phone you use.

Signing in

I didn't get the confirmation email. It comes from noreply@talvii.co.uk, so check your spam or junk folder. On the "Check your email" screen, tap Resend email to send another. Once you tap the link, the app signs you in by itself.

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password?. The email links to a page where you choose a new one, then sign in with it in the app.

Using Talvii

I forgot to tick something off yesterday. Tap that day on the grid on Today or on a habit's page. You can fix any past day, but not future ones.

I'm going on holiday or I'm ill. Go to Settings → Take a break to pause every habit, or pause one habit from its page. Paused days don't count against your score or streaks, and reminders stop.

I want to stop a habit but keep its history. Edit the habit and choose Archive. Archived habits are listed on the Habits screen, where you can restore them.

How is the score worked out? On Today, tap How is this calculated? under your score.

Reminders

Reminders aren't arriving. Check that notifications are allowed for Talvii in your phone's settings, and that the habit has a reminder time. Reminders are skipped on days you've already done the habit, on paused days, and on days it isn't due.

Your phone keeps about the next two weeks of reminders, and Talvii tops them up each time you open it. If you haven't opened the app for a while, open it once to get them going again.

Your devices and data

Using more than one device. Sign in with the same account and your habits sync between them. Talvii also works offline; changes are sent when you're back online.

Getting a copy of your data. Go to Settings → Export your data for spreadsheet (CSV) files of your habits, history and notes.

Deleting your account. Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it straight away. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you.
